## About me

I'm a senior engineer with **10 years of experience** across frontend architecture, cross-platform products, and full-stack delivery. My recent work focuses on **AI-native products**: multi-agent workflows, multi-model gateways, streaming voice, digital humans, and native capability orchestration.

I care about the parts between the boxes—typed boundaries, failure recovery, observability, and the product details that make ambitious systems feel dependable.
